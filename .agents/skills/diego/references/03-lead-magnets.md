# Module 3 — Lead Magnets

## Main principle

The lead magnet should attract the buyer of the paid offer, not simply generate the cheapest or largest lead volume.

## Net vs filter

Diego connects lead magnet breadth to service delivery load.

### Lower delivery load / highly scalable products
The lead magnet can act more like a **net** and capture a broader audience.

### Higher delivery load / DFY or high-touch delivery
The lead magnet should act more like a **filter** and pre-qualify the prospect.

## Common mismatch

A service provider often copies lead magnets used by infoproduct marketers. This can attract people who want to learn to do the task themselves rather than people who want to hire a provider.

### Example logic

DIY framing:
“How to optimize X yourself.”

Service-buyer framing:
“How we achieved Y for a client / how to identify whether X is costing you customers.”

The underlying subject may overlap; the positioning changes who is attracted.

## Consumption speed

Lead magnets should be high quality and easy to consume. Very long courses can delay buying because prospects may decide to “finish the free course first.”

## Types discussed

- ebooks / guides,
- templates,
- video lessons / webinars / VSLs,
- free trials,
- tools,
- AI assistants,
- calculators,
- checklists,
- short courses / minicourses,
- quizzes,
- case studies,
- communities / exclusive groups.

## Diego's preferred characteristics

He especially favors lead magnets that are:

- actionable,
- easy to consume,
- capable of giving a quick tangible result or insight.

Tools, calculators, checklists, and other immediately usable assets fit this well.

## Minicourse use case

Diego is more favorable toward short courses when they naturally create the next problem solved by a recurring membership/product. He is less favorable when a long course unnecessarily delays purchase of a service.

## Case studies for DFY

Case studies are highlighted as particularly strong for DFY services because they demonstrate capability and outcome without necessarily training the prospect to replace the service provider.

## AI usage

AI can help:

- identify customer problems,
- generate lead magnet ideas,
- create variations,
- outline content.

Diego warns against blindly publishing the first AI-generated output as the brand's first contact with a prospect. The final asset should be reviewed/improved by someone with domain understanding.

## Lead magnet selection checklist

1. What are we selling: DFY, DWY, DIY?
2. How much delivery load does a new customer create?
3. Does this asset attract the actual buyer of the offer?
4. Can it be consumed quickly?
5. Is it actionable?
6. Does it produce a tangible insight/result?
7. Does it delay the purchase unnecessarily?
