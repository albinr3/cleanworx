# Module 1 — Content Strategy

## Main objective of content

Diego frames content primarily as a tool to:

- build audiences,
- generate and capture demand,
- multiply advertising effectiveness,
- build authority,
- create connection/familiarity,
- create reciprocity and loyalty,
- nurture prospects so buying becomes easier.

He explicitly warns against creating content without knowing the business objective, because large audiences can still be difficult to monetize.

## Capture demand vs generate demand

### Capture demand
Reach people already looking for a solution. Search-driven platforms are strongest here.

### Generate demand
Put a relevant idea/solution in front of people who were not actively searching but could become buyers.

YouTube is presented as especially valuable because it can do both.

## Audience quality

Diego argues that audience quality is driven substantially by:

- time spent with the creator/business,
- knowledge of the creator's beliefs, values, skills, services, and proof.

This supports his preference for long-form content.

## Platform logic

### YouTube
Preferred starting platform for many service businesses under roughly €10k/month in his model.
Reasons taught in the source:

- long-form nurturing,
- search/SEO,
- stronger community/familiarity,
- ability to redirect traffic,
- videos can remain useful for a long time,
- long-form can be repurposed into short-form.

### Instagram
Useful for stories, DMs, automation, connection, and retargeting. Content is more transient.

### TikTok
Strong discovery/recommendation and increasingly searchable, but Diego considers followers/community less durable and the ad ecosystem weaker for buyer identification than Meta/Google.

### LinkedIn
Presented as a strong B2B opportunity, though Diego explicitly says his direct experience there was lower at the time.

## Scale-based content model taught in the video

- Under roughly €10k/month: prioritize YouTube + lead funnel + modest paid acquisition.
- Around €12k–€25k/month: keep YouTube and hire an editor to create vertical clips.
- Around €25k–€50k/month: adapt content natively to each platform rather than merely reposting identical clips.
- Above roughly €50k/month: increase distribution with team/subaccounts/systems.

These are Diego's suggested thresholds, not universal laws.

## What to create

Core rule: create content that solves real problems of the target audience.

Problem discovery sources mentioned:

- direct market/client experience,
- competitor content,
- Google/autocomplete,
- AnswerThePublic,
- Quora,
- TikTok/search behavior,
- AI-assisted problem extraction.

## B2B qualification through content

For B2B, specific content can pre-qualify the viewer. A narrow operational topic may attract fewer views but a more commercially relevant audience than a broad “how to grow” topic.

## Short-form role

In Diego's model, short-form often functions as discovery/distribution, with the goal of moving qualified people toward long-form content.

Examples:

short-form → CTA / keyword / ManyChat / profile link → YouTube long-form.

## Mistakes highlighted

- optimizing primarily for views,
- never starting,
- inconsistency,
- perfectionism,
- attracting the wrong audience with entertainment/clickbait unrelated to the buyer.

## Practical interpretation

When advising a service business, prefer:

customer problem → useful specific content → qualified attention → long-form nurturing → offer/funnel.
