# Module 8 — Waitlist / Demand Compression Strategy

## Preconditions

Diego says this strategy is difficult without at least one of:

- meaningful authority,
- documented case studies/results,
- suppressed demand from previous campaigns/launch activity.

These can overlap.

## Genuine scarcity

The strategy depends on veracity. Capacity limits, remaining places, deadlines, and waitlist counts must be real.

Do not recommend fake scarcity.

## Demand > capacity changes the sales dynamic

If more qualified people want access than the business can responsibly serve, the business can become more selective and reduce dependence on persuasion-heavy selling.

## Capacity protects service quality

Diego describes cancelling/rejecting calls when taking more customers would reduce client results. Capacity is therefore both an operations constraint and a legitimate source of scarcity.

## “Path of veracity”

Diego describes building credibility through a sequence where the business publicly states things it can track and later demonstrates that they happened.

Concept:

credible statement/prediction → result → audience observes consistency → next claim becomes more believable.

This requires reliable tracking.

## Numbers to know before the campaign

Ideally track:

- leads/day,
- booking rate,
- show rate,
- close rate,
- capacity,
- waitlist size,
- conversion by source.

## Prepare a traffic event

Before opening access, prepare a meaningful inflow of qualified traffic, such as:

- strong organic content,
- podcast/collaboration,
- paid placement,
- paid acquisition,
- multiple high-potential content assets.

## Waitlist nurturing

Do not leave the list idle.

Use:

- email nurturing,
- YouTube/long-form,
- repeated useful touchpoints,
- omnichannel reinforcement.

Diego explicitly connects this to his validated nurturing flow from the email lesson.

## Communicate demand vs capacity

Before opening, communicate the real ratio between interested people and available capacity multiple times so prospects understand the constraint.

## Opening / closing

Open the stated number of places, communicate remaining availability truthfully, and close when actual capacity is filled.

Do not extend fake deadlines or invent last-minute capacity merely to sell more.

## Campaign assets can become evergreen retargeting

Diego describes a sequence of content assets (“acts”) that document progressive results. After the campaign, these can be reused in retargeting/video sequences.

Example logic:

Asset 1 → viewers reaching a threshold → Asset 2 → engaged viewers → Asset 3.

## Demand-compression model

Instead of selling every lead immediately:

collect → nurture → accumulate demand → open limited capacity → convert concentrated demand.

Use only when the preconditions are real.
