**Business:** CleanWorx Auto Detailing & Ceramic Coatings

**Primary service area:** Basking Ridge, NJ, within a 20–25 mile radius  
**Source:** `keyword research CLEANWORX.csv` (115 keywords; 3,005,680 reported monthly searches)

## Mapping rules

- Search volume is a national estimate and is **not additive** across close variants. Local conversion intent and keyword difficulty determine priority.
- The homepage owns broad local detailing intent. Each service gets one canonical landing page; variants are covered with headings, service copy, FAQs, and internal anchors rather than thin duplicate pages.
- Use Basking Ridge and the verified service area in title tags, copy, LocalBusiness schema, Google Business Profile, and internal links. Do not produce city pages until each has 600+ words and genuinely unique local proof.
- Do not target **premium car wash**: CleanWorx explicitly differentiates itself from a regular car wash. The phrase `car waxing` is permissible only as a secondary feature of an exterior-detailing service.

## Build order and acceptance criteria

1. Optimize `/` and publish `/ceramic-coating`, `/paint-correction`, `/interior-detailing`, `/exterior-detailing`, and `/mobile-auto-detailing` first.
2. Publish the P1 pages next, starting with `/ceramic-coating-cost` because it captures high-conversion price research without competing with the core coating page.
3. Each core service page requires: unique 800+ word copy, original job photos, clear Basking Ridge/service-area copy, a booking CTA, Service + LocalBusiness schema, and internal links listed above.
4. The homepage should use LocalBusiness + Organization schema. Do not publish scaled city-service pages without the local uniqueness requirements in the SEO plan.
5. Track calls, booking-form submissions, appointments, organic landing-page sessions, and local-pack visibility per canonical URL. Review search-query overlap after 60–90 days and consolidate only if URLs clearly compete.
