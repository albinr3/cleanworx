# Behaviors & Interaction Audit: AutoDetail Homepage 2

Target: `https://reactheme.com/products/wordpress/autodetail/homepage-2/`

## 1. Top Notice Bar
- Dismissible floating/pinned announcement bar with gradient: `linear-gradient(260deg, #0170B9 0%, #0D093B 100%)`.
- Dismissible on clicking close button (X).

## 2. Header & Navigation
- Sticky header on scroll with glassmorphism / dark background (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.95)` with backdrop blur).
- Responsive mobile off-canvas drawer with hamburger icon and smooth slide-in.
- Search trigger overlay modal.
- Active state indicator and hover transitions on navigation links.
- "Make Appointment" CTA button with hover animation.

## 3. Hero Section
- Background video: looping muted automotive detailing video (`/images/autodetail/hero-video.mp4`).
- Dark radial gradient overlay for high contrast text readability.
- Headline: "Luxury Detail", subtext: "AutoDetail offers efficient, reliable detailing that restores shine and preserves your vehicle."
- Interactive CTA: "Make Appointment".

## 4. Brand Partner Strip
- Infinite/smooth scrolling client marquee displaying automotive manufacturers:
  Audi, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Rolls-Royce, Renault, Peugeot, Mitsubishi, etc.
  (Using `/images/autodetail/1.webp` through `16.webp`).

## 5. About Us Section
- Dual staggered imagery (`/images/autodetail/p1.webp` and `/images/autodetail/p2.webp`).
- Key values, detailer statistics counter (Hours worked, Happy customers, Vehicles protected, Years of experience).
- "Read More" and feature bullet checkmarks.

## 6. Services Grid
- 6 interactive service cards with hover image zoom, red accent tags, and "Learn More" links.
- Numbers: 01 to 06 with badge layout.

## 7. What They Say (Testimonials)
- Swiper / Carousel with authentic customer reviews, star ratings, and Google Review badge (`google-icon.svg`).
- Interactive navigation dots/arrows.

## 8. Call to Action Banner
- Full-width crimson red (`#e30613`) banner with appointment booking callout.

## 9. Why Choose Us Grid
- 4 feature cards highlighting Expert Technicians, Tailored Packages, Affordable Pricing, Aftercare Support.

## 10. Cockpit Visual Separator
- Parallax / high-definition visual banner using `/images/autodetail/c1-scaled.webp`.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions
- Interactive accordion with 5 questions, smooth height expand/collapse, and rotating chevron icon.

## 12. Instagram Gallery
- 8 square photos with hover dark overlay, zoom effect, and Instagram camera icon.

## 13. Footer & Back-to-Top
- Comprehensive 4-column footer with brand info, quick links, services, contact details, social links, and floating back-to-top scroll button.
