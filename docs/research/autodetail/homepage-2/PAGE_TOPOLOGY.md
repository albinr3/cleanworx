# Page Topology: AutoDetail Homepage 2

Target URL: `https://reactheme.com/products/wordpress/autodetail/homepage-2/`
Destination: `src/app/page.tsx`

## Section Hierarchy & Components
1. `<NoticeBar />` - Top dismissible notice bar (`RTNB` style)
2. `<Header />` - Sticky navigation with logo, nav links, phone, search modal trigger, mobile menu drawer, and appointment CTA
3. `<HeroSection />` - Video background hero (`/images/autodetail/hero-video.mp4`) with typography & appointment CTA
4. `<BrandPartners />` - Auto manufacturer logo carousel (16 brand logos)
5. `<AboutSection />` - Dual image layout, story, metrics, bullet points, and CTA
6. `<StatsCounters />` - 4 key business counters
7. `<ServicesSection />` - 6 car detailing service cards with images & pricing
8. `<WhyChooseUs />` - 4 value proposition cards
9. `<CockpitBanner />` - Parallax luxury cockpit interior visual break
10. `<TestimonialsSection />` - Client reviews carousel with avatars & Google ratings
11. `<CtaBanner />` - Full-width high-contrast red CTA banner
12. `<FaqSection />` - 5 interactive accordion questions
13. `<InstagramGallery />` - 8-image Instagram showcase grid (`@autodetail_works`)
14. `<Footer />` - Multi-column footer with contact info & social links
15. `<AppointmentModal />` - Interactive booking dialog when clicking "Make Appointment"
16. `<BackToTop />` - Floating smooth scroll-to-top button
