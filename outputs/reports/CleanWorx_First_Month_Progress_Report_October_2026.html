Detailed breakdown of work completed

Migration & Authority Preservation Phases 1 through 5

143 Legacy URLs Audited and Mapped with 301 Redirects

Audited all 143 legacy URLs from previous XML sitemaps and Search Console, structuring a direct 1:1 server-level redirect map to preserve historical search authority and prevent ranking loss.

  • Locked in the pre-migration baseline and archived historical queries driving search impressions.
  • Documented all 143 legacy paths in a redirect ledger mapping each old address directly to its new destination.
  • Consolidated duplicate city pages and thin service pages into main service hubs.
  • Configured and verified 143 direct server-level 301 rules without redirect hops or intermediate chains.
  • Ran static export and pre-launch quality audits to verify zero broken links and zero 404 errors.

Built a custom website using Next.js and React with clean layouts, quick load speeds, and straightforward mobile booking.
