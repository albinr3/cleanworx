Add-On & Upgrade FAQs

Clear answers regarding add-on compatibility, durability, and scheduling.

Can I book an add-on service by itself without getting a full detail?

Yes. Headlight Restoration ($125 standalone), Engine Bay Cleaning ($125 standalone), Air Purification ($125 standalone), and Window Tint Removal (from $20 per window) can be booked separately. Ask about in-shop or mobile availability for your service.

What is the difference between a 1-step polish and full paint correction?

A 1-step machine polish uses a single finishing pad and fine compound to clean paint pores and boost optical gloss, removing 40%–60% of very light hazing. Full multi-stage paint correction starts at $350 and involves heavy compounding followed by fine polishing to eliminate 85% to 90%+ of deep swirl marks and scratches.

How long will my headlight restoration last?

Unlike quick DIY kits that fog up in weeks, our professional service concludes with a multi-year ceramic coating that chemically bonds with the freshly polished polycarbonate. This provides up to 2 years of UV resistance against sun oxidation and winter road salts.

Will window tint removal damage my rear window defrosters?

No. We use specialized high-temperature steam and non-caustic adhesive softeners that allow the tint to release gently. We never use razor blades on rear windows equipped with embedded defroster lines or radio antennas.

How does the pet hair and heavy soil surcharge work?

Standard interior detailing covers routine soil and vacuuming. When pet hair is deeply woven into automotive carpets or beach sand is compacted into floor fibers, mechanical extraction tools are required. We inspect the vehicle upon arrival and confirm the exact fee (typically $50 to $150) before touching the vehicle.
