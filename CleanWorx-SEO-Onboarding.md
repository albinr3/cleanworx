## Business Overview

CleanWorx provides both mobile and in-shop interior and exterior vehicle cleaning and detailing. Mobile services are performed at the client’s home or workplace. The business specializes in 3-, 6-, and 9-year ceramic-coating protection and also offers paint correction to remove scratches and spiderwebbing, engine-bay cleaning, foggy-headlight restoration, stain removal, steam cleaning, and mold and odor removal.

### Main Services

- Ceramic coatings
- Full details
- Engine-bay cleaning
- Odor removal

### Priority & Most Profitable Services

Ceramic coatings are the highest-ticket service and should be the leading SEO priority, followed by exterior details.

### Differentiators

Most detailing businesses last only about three years; CleanWorx has operated for nearly a decade. The business emphasizes going above and beyond for customers and providing superior communication.
